Anonymous audio listening study

Thank you for helping. This is a short, voluntary research study that compares short computer-generated audio clips against a written text description.

  • Expected time: about 15–20 minutes.
  • You will hear pairs of audio clips (A and B) and say which better matches a text prompt.
  • Participation is voluntary; you may stop at any time by closing the tab.
  • Responses are anonymous and may be used in scientific research and publication.
  • We collect no names, emails, health information, location, or other sensitive personal data. No cookies or analytics are used.
  • Please use headphones in a quiet room, and set a comfortable, fixed volume that you do not change during the study.

By continuing you confirm that you are 18 or older and consent to participate on these terms.

Before you begin

  1. Put on headphones and play the sample below to set a comfortable, fixed listening level. Keep this level for the whole study.
  2. On each trial you will see a text prompt and two clips, A and B. You may replay each clip once.
  3. Answer two questions per trial: which clip better matches the text (main question), and which has better overall sound quality.
  4. There is no right pace — but please answer based on your first careful impression.
